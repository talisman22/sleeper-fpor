Each player is measured against the players drafted around him who fill the same lineup spots: QBs vs QBs, RB/WR/TE together (flex), K vs K, DEF vs DEF. Replacement is their average points per game played, so missed games never count against anyone. Undrafted pickups are measured against the end of the draft.
